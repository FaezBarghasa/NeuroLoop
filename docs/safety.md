# NeuroLoop - Safety & Wellness Disclaimer

## 1. Wellness Tool - Not a Medical Device
NeuroLoop is an experimental wellness application intended for relaxation, meditation, sleep habit support, and focus assistance. It is **not** a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, neurological disorder, or sleep pathology.

---

## 2. Precautions & Contraindications
> [!CAUTION]
> **Photosensitive Epilepsy & Seizures**: Brainwave entrainment (especially isochronic pulses and high-frequency beta/gamma rhythms) may trigger adverse neurological events in individuals predisposed to epilepsy or seizure disorders. Do not use this application if you have a history of epilepsy or unexplained seizures.

- **Operating Machinery & Driving**: Never listen to sleep, relaxation, or deep delta/theta presets while driving, cycling, or operating heavy machinery.
- **Auditory Health**: Maintain comfortable listening volumes (below 65 dBA). Binaural and isochronic effects do not require high volume to be perceived by the auditory cortex.
- **Discomfort Feedback**: If you experience headaches, nausea, or anxiety during a session, immediately stop playback and log a "discomfort" rating so the self-tuning engine permanently reduces the weight and intensity of that frequency.
